Reading assemblies for schools

If you want students to be fired up about reading, you need more than an ordinary assembly. Books burst to life through magic, storytelling and nonstop audience participation.

The Marvelous Magical Stories Show

Any time of year: book fairs, reading nights and more. Grades K to 5.

Beloved stories leap off the page as Dean blends astonishing magic with reading themed lessons. The featured books rotate often, so the show stays fresh. Titles have included The Invention of Hugo Cabret, The Magician's Elephant, The Wartville Wizard, The Fantastic Flying Books of Mr. Morris Lessmore and A Fish Out of Water.

The Seusstacular Reading Show

Perfect for Read Across America Week, literacy nights and book fairs.

For over 25 years Dean has presented shows about the books and stories of Dr. Seuss, and they have been extremely popular. The Cat in the Hat, Horton and more, all brought to life with magic, laughter and pure Seussical energy. Others have tried to copy it. Go with the originator.
