Library magic shows

Family shows filled with magic and story references, for children's programs, Library Week, Read Across America and summer reading.

The Marvelous Magical Stories Show

Any time of year. The ideal show for Library Week.

Beloved stories leap off the page as Dean blends astonishing magic with reading themed lessons. The featured books rotate often, so the show stays fresh. Titles have included The Invention of Hugo Cabret, The Magician's Elephant, The Wartville Wizard, The Fantastic Flying Books of Mr. Morris Lessmore and A Fish Out of Water.

The Seusstacular Reading Show

Spring, for Read Across America Week and Dr. Seuss's birthday, and any time of year.

For over 25 years Dean has presented shows about the books and stories of Dr. Seuss, and they have been extremely popular. The Cat in the Hat, Horton and more, all brought to life with magic, laughter and pure Seussical energy. Others have tried to copy it. Go with the originator.

Seasonal shows

Through the year, Dean presents a show for every season.

October

Fall-O-Ween Magic

Festive and fun, with nothing scary. Tricks with pumpkins, friendly ghosts, and a fellow named Mr. Bones who can never seem to put himself together correctly. Perfectly silly and filled with laughter, just the way young kids like it.

November

Turkey-Kadabra

Magic, comedy and audience participation with fascinating stories about the Mayflower, the Pilgrims, turkeys and more. The perfect mix of fun and learning for a Thanksgiving celebration.

Winter

The VERY Frozen Magic Show

A fun and festive show to close out the year or start the new one, with magic featuring Frosty, penguins, Frozen characters and modern classics like Snowmen at Night.

Summer

A NEW show every summer

A brand new show each year for your summer reading program, filled with magic and story references for family audiences. Ask about this summer's show.
